About

Emad Basma

With over 20 years of experience in Tracy, CA, and beyond, Emad has built a reputation as a trusted real estate expert. As a top producer for over a decade, he specializes in helping buyers and sellers navigate the competitive markets of Tracy, Dublin, Livermore, Manteca, Lathrop, and the Bay Area. His deep knowledge of the local market and commitment to excellence ensure that every client receives expert guidance and exceptional service.

Emad's top priority is delivering a white-glove experience, where every client feels valued, informed, and confident in their real estate decisions. Whether buying or selling, he is dedicated to making the process seamless and rewarding. With Emad, clients can trust that their home journey is in the best hands.
