## Visual brand (from the truck, the signs, the logo — not invented)
- **Logo:** `reference/legacy-site/assets/agent4lifelogo.png` (636×112, transparent). A white "4" mark with a red diagonal slash, then **AGENT** in red, **4 LIFE** in white. Built for dark backgrounds. Keep it exactly. Logo colors: white `#FFFFFF`, red `#BF0811`.
- **Palette that already exists in the world:** truck black, LED red, white. Site signs use a deeper crimson (~`#A8202B`). So: near-black ground for hero/footer, white paper for reading sections, one red. No second accent, no gold, no navy gradients.
- **Type direction:** the logo is a wide geometric sans. Pair a heavy grotesk display (Archivo Black / Archivo at 800, or Sora 800) with a plain workhorse body (Inter or Archivo). No serif — that's Jack's brand, not Emad's.
