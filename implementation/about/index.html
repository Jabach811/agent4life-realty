Before real estate, Emad served for 22 years as a Santa Clara County deputy sheriff.

That experience shaped his approach to representing clients: honest communication, careful attention, and responsibility for the work. His own homebuying experiences inspired him to enter real estate in 2004.

He works primarily with sellers and also represents buyers with a range of goals. His experience includes equity sales, foreclosures, and short sales. He is a Certified Distressed Property Expert and a graduate of California State University, Chico.

As the founder of Agent4Life, he leads a team that includes his son, Nizar, and daughter, Sonya. Together, they help clients navigate their next move with a personal approach.
