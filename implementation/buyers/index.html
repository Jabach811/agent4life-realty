From the first look
to the keys.

  1. Set the brief.

    Separate essentials from nice-to-haves. Consider location, space, daily routines, and your timing.

  2. Talk with a lender early.

    If you plan to finance, discuss your budget and loan options with a lender. Ask Emad for an introduction if you need a starting point.

  3. Visit and compare.

    Look beyond photos. Take time to consider condition, layout, and how each property matches your priorities.

  4. Prepare an offer.

    Work through price, proposed timing, and terms with your agent before making a decision.

  5. Follow through carefully.

    Stay engaged with inspections, lender requests, and the agreed deadlines. Ask questions whenever something is unclear.

  6. Plan the move.

    Coordinate the final walkthrough, closing, and practical details of your move with the people involved.
